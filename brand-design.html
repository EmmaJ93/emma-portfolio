01 · Brand System

The rules behind every piece

TandemKross's look is built on a short list of rules: the bracketed [TK] mark, a red, green and off-black palette, SF Proverbial Gothic display type, and real products photographed in real environments. I helped define those standards and built the 2023 brand guidelines document that records them. Everything else on this page follows it.

I've since started taking the brand to new audiences beyond core rimfire shooters, including the lifestyle space and the broader 2A community, while keeping the same standards.

  • ClientTandemKross, rimfire parts & accessories (Weare, NH)
  • CoversLogo variations, primary/secondary palette, color rules, type, motifs, imagery
  • GrowthExtending the brand into lifestyle and the broader 2A community

02 · Product Launches

Launch systems: Manticore X, ADX and the TKX22 Light Rifle

Each launch gets a small visual system that I take across every channel: a hero banner for the website, email headers and modules, feature callouts and product renders, and a print-ready spec sheet for dealers.

For Manticore X, the campaign is built on three words, Ergonomic, Modular and Versatile, set in one consistent frame, so each email and banner reinforces the same message. ADX gets its own bold treatment, carried from the homepage hero through to an "Owner's Club" email header.
