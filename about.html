A little about me

My favorite space is where technology and art blend to bring concepts to life. I'm detail-oriented, creative, and personable, with an eye for fresh ideas and the technical skill to make them real.

I trained as a photojournalist at Photography Studies College in Melbourne, and I've spent more than twelve years behind the camera since: shooting weddings, portraits, and commercial work, then designing the albums and editing the films that tell those stories.

Today I'm the Marketing Specialist at TANDEMKROSS, where I create all of the company's photography and run email marketing, including automated customer journeys. Day to day that means product photography and video, graphics and layouts in Adobe Creative Suite, 3D work in Cinema 4D, persuasive copy, social media, and the occasional bit of code for the website.

I care about work that looks good and does its job, and about the people I make it with.
