01 · The Program

A large list built entirely from organic traffic.

TandemKross makes aftermarket parts and accessories for rimfire firearms. Firearm-industry rules mean no paid advertising to collect contacts and no SMS, so every subscriber comes from organic site traffic and on-site sign-up forms.

I moved the program from Mailchimp to Klaviyo in 2025, rebuilt the automated emails as segmented customer journeys, and run a full campaign calendar of launches, restocks, and holiday promotions.
